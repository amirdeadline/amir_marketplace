# No-Slop Writing

Generate or rewrite the requested text using the following rules.

## Core requirements

* Preserve all facts, meaning, constraints, names, dates, numbers, and technical details.
* Do not invent facts, examples, results, quotes, experiences, or opinions.
* Write like a knowledgeable human speaking clearly to another human.
* Prefer direct, specific language over polished-sounding filler.
* Match the intended audience and context.
* Do not make the text artificially casual or intentionally imperfect.
* Humanized means natural and specific, not grammatically incorrect.

## Remove AI-writing patterns

Do not use:

* exaggerated praise
* generic introductions
* unnecessary summaries
* motivational conclusions
* repetitive conclusions
* fake quotations
* empty transitions
* excessive headings
* excessive bullet lists
* repeated restatements of the same point
* unnecessary three-item lists
* unnecessary em dashes
* rhetorical questions unless they serve a real purpose
* phrases such as “let’s dive in,” “it is important to note,” “in today’s rapidly evolving world,” or “this is a game changer”

Avoid vague words when a concrete statement is possible, including:

* comprehensive
* robust
* seamless
* innovative
* transformative
* cutting-edge
* pivotal
* holistic
* leverage
* harness
* delve
* landscape
* ecosystem
* journey
* unlock

These words are allowed only when they carry necessary technical meaning.

## Sentence style

* Vary sentence length naturally.
* Use contractions when appropriate.
* Prefer active voice, but do not force it.
* Start with the actual point.
* Use plain words where they are equally accurate.
* Keep technical terminology when it improves precision.
* Do not explain obvious information.
* Do not sound promotional unless promotional writing was requested.

## Editing procedure

1. Identify the intended meaning and audience.
2. Remove filler, repetition, clichés, and unsupported claims.
3. Replace vague claims with concrete facts already present.
4. Improve sentence rhythm and natural transitions.
5. Check that no facts or requirements were lost.
6. Perform a final no-slop audit before returning the result.

## Output rules

* Return only the finished text unless analysis was explicitly requested.
* Do not describe the editing process.
* Do not claim the text is undetectable as AI-generated.
* Do not optimize for AI detectors.
* Optimize for accuracy, clarity, specificity, and natural human communication.
